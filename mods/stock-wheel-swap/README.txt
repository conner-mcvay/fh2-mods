FORZA HORIZON 2 (PC) - STOCK WHEEL SWAP
=======================================

Every car's stock wheels become wheel options for any car: put 2000GT wheels on
a Mustang, F40 wheels on a Golf. They're in the wheel shop next to the
aftermarket brands, filed under the car's brand (Toyota, Ferrari, ...).

Built for game version 1.0.0.64 (Anthem_1.0.0.64_x64__8wekyb3d8bbwe).

What it changes in gamedbRC.slt (List_Wheels, 344 stock wheels):
  - IsStock 1 -> 0, so the wheel shop lists them
  - PartManufacturerID -> the car brand (from the MediaName prefix: TOY -> Toyota,
    POR -> Porsche, ...). Bowler, Donkervoort and Local Motors have no brand
    entry in the game and show as "Stock"
  - DisplayName -> year + the game's own name for the car, e.g. "1969 2000GT"
  - Price -> by weight, on the same scale as the aftermarket wheels
    (3,200 - 3,800 CR)
  - WheelAnnotations: one row per wheel, so the wheel shop lists it. Category by
    the Autoshow group of its car: Sport (sports cars, hot hatches, track toys,
    rally), the forged category (supercars, GT cars, luxury SUVs) or
    Specialized (classics, muscle, offroad)
Each car still starts on its own stock wheels.

The wheel shop loads every non-stock wheel from
media\cars\_library\scene\wheels\<wheel>.zip, but stock wheels only exist inside
their car's zip. So the build makes one wheel zip per wheel from your own game
files (the car's front wheel model and its AO texture; the mod ships no game
assets), registers them in zipmanifest.xml and Mount\hash.manifest, and the
installer patches azimuth.exe for the new index. imports.txt and
index-donors.txt are generated by Generate-Imports.ps1.

Known gaps: the wheel shop thumbnails are blank (they're pre-rendered images in
ui\textures\data_bound\Upgrade_Parts.zip). A few wheels use textures from their
car's own folder (e.g. center-cap logos), which may not show on other cars.

Not changed: traffic and placeholder wheels, and 169 leftover wheel entries from
older Forza games whose cars (and wheel models) aren't in FH2.
